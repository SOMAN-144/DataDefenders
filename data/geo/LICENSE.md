# Map data licence and attribution

`india_states.geojson` is derived from the **DataMeet Community Maps Project**, *India - State Boundaries*
(`States/Admin2`): https://projects.datameet.org/maps/states

- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 India (CC BY 2.5 IN).
- **Changes made:** geometries simplified with Shapely (tolerance 0.012 degrees, 0.002 for small territories), coordinates rounded to 4 decimals, and `state_code`, `state_name` and label points added.
- **Unchanged original:** `data/raw/geo_datameet_states/`.

**Disclaimer.** These are community-sourced boundaries, simplified for web display. They are indicative only and are not an authoritative depiction of India's external or internal boundaries. For official maps, refer to the Survey of India. Anyone publishing this project more widely should review the boundaries against India's mapping rules first.
